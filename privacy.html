Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Zetsu StickerZ (Sticker Mania) is a digital sticker vending and collection experience operated by The Zetsumetsu Corporation (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy describes the information used by the Service, why it is used, when it may be visible to others, and how users can request access to or deletion of their information.

1. Information we collect or process

  • Discord account information: When you choose Discord sign-in, the Service receives account identifiers and profile information made available through that authorization, such as your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar. We use an internal account identifier to link your profile to your StickerZ account.
  • Capsules and collection activity: We keep records of capsule purchases, selected rarity, verified purchase status, collection contents, and related activity, including timestamps and identifiers needed to prevent duplicate fulfillment.
  • Payment and blockchain information: For purchases using SOL or $AWT (Artworqq Token), when a payment option is supported at checkout, transaction references, payment amounts, public wallet addresses or transaction signatures may be processed to verify payment and deliver the correct reward. Blockchain transaction information is generally public.
  • Browser data: Your browser may retain a sign-in session, pending-purchase details, and your volume preference in local storage. This helps maintain your session and recover purchases after a redirect or reload.
  • Technical information: Hosting, authentication, security, blockchain, and market-data providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device details, and request logs when their services are accessed.

We do not ask for your Discord password, wallet recovery phrase, or private wallet key. Do not share those credentials with anyone claiming to represent StickerZ.

2. How the information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, display their profile, associate stickers with the correct account, verify cryptocurrency payments, fulfill purchased capsules, present purchase and collection history, recover interrupted transactions, detect misuse, troubleshoot failures, and maintain the security and operation of Sticker Mania.

Discord account information is used for the account and game features described here, not to profile Discord users for unrelated advertising or eligibility decisions.

3. Information visible to other users

Sticker Mania currently includes a public “Recent Buys” log. It may show a buyer's display name, the sticker obtained, and its capsule rarity. Your own account page also shows your purchases and collectibles when you are signed in. Public Solana transactions can be viewed independently of the Service through blockchain explorers.

Do not assume that a public transaction can be reversed or erased from the Solana blockchain.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use third-party services to operate StickerZ, including Discord for login; Supabase for authentication, account data, collection records, and payment verification; GitHub Pages for website hosting; and Solana infrastructure and wallet providers for blockchain transactions. The browser may also contact cryptocurrency price-data services such as Coinbase, Kraken, or CoinGecko to display a SOL price.

Relevant information is transmitted to these providers only as needed for those services, or otherwise when required by law or directed by the user. Their own privacy policies may apply to information they process independently. We do not sell Discord API account data or provide it to advertising networks or data brokers.

5. Storage, security, and retention

Account and purchase data are managed through the Service's backend, including authenticated access controls. No online service can guarantee complete security. You should protect your Discord login and wallet credentials.

We keep account and collectible records while needed to provide the Service, investigate payments, protect against abuse, and satisfy applicable legal obligations. Data that is no longer needed should be deleted or de-identified where feasible. Some records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance. Information written to a public blockchain is not controlled by us and cannot ordinarily be deleted from that blockchain.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may disconnect access to the app through Discord's authorized-app settings. You may also clear locally stored browser data, but clearing it can remove your saved session and pending-purchase recovery details. Disconnecting Discord or clearing browser storage does not automatically delete records already held in the StickerZ backend.

To request access, correction, or deletion of account-associated information, contact the Zetsu StickerZ operator through an official support channel. An initial request can be opened at the Zetsu StickerZ GitHub issue tracker, asking for a private way to continue the request. GitHub issues are public: do not post your Discord user ID, wallet address, payment information, or other sensitive personal information there. Ownership of an account may need to be verified before changes are made. We will handle requests subject to applicable law and necessary security or transaction-record retention.

7. Age and platform requirements

Users must meet Discord's minimum age and eligibility rules, any higher age threshold applicable in their jurisdiction, and the relevant wallet or payment provider requirements. The Service is not intended to collect personal information from children who are not eligible to use the platforms involved.

8. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when features, supported payment methods, or data practices change. A new effective date will appear on this page. New uses of personal information will be disclosed as required by applicable law.

9. Operator and contact

The Zetsumetsu Corporation operates Zetsu StickerZ as part of its multimedia experience ecosystem. For general questions, use the project support issue tracker without including private details, and request a private contact method for privacy-specific concerns.
